Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

1. Who we are

Fact Video Bot is a personal tool operated by atanasovvw.

2. What data we use

When the owner connects their TikTok account, the app receives an access token that allows it to upload videos to that account. The app does not collect, store, or share any personal data about other people.

3. How data is used

The access token is used only to upload videos to the owner's own TikTok account. It is stored locally on the owner's computer and is never sold, shared, or published.

4. Third parties

The app uses TikTok's official API and Anthropic's Claude API (to write video scripts). No personal data from TikTok users is sent to Anthropic.

5. Data retention and deletion

The owner can remove the app's access at any time in TikTok settings (Settings and privacy > Security and permissions > Apps and services). Deleting the local settings file removes the stored token.
